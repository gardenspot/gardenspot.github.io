Disclaimer: Generation Constraints
  • Cube Size: The puzzle always forms a perfect 3x3x3 solid cube (27 total blocks).
  • Piece Count: The puzzle is strictly generated with exactly 5 or 6 distinct interlocking pieces.
  • Minimum Size: Every piece is strictly guaranteed to have at least 3 blocks.
  • True 3D (Red Buttons): Generates pieces that are impossible to model using a single 2D sketch + CADD extrusion.

Computing Puzzle...

Finding a combination where all pieces meet the strict constraints. This requires searching thousands of possible geometries.
